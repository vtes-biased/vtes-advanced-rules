## 1. Reducing stealth or intercept before any block attempt

**The question.** May a card or ability that reduces the acting minion's stealth (or a blocker's intercept) be used
before any block attempt exists: by a third party, by an effect usable "during the action", or by the acting minion?

**The record.**

- Groups "Minus stealth" (G00104) and "Minus Intercept" (G00102): "Can be played/used any time during the action before
  resolution; a block attempt is not required." [LSJ 20020612](https://usenet.krcg.org/t/sC0pTtJJDj4/#m2) (original:
  "Does a block attempt even have to be made…? No!" — "Correct.").
- Group "Reactions targeting another player" (G00103) [ANK 20200607]: other Methuselahs still pass the impulse in order.

**The owner's reading.** A third party stripping stealth "needs the defender to attempt first" ([rules-qa
1.3](https://github.com/lionel-panhaleux/zero-vtes/blob/main/docs/rules-qa.md#batch-1--sequencing-impulse-windows)); "an
effect usable during the action (Screamer's and Aranthebes's -1 stealth …) waits for the block attempts" ([rules-qa
66.1](https://github.com/lionel-panhaleux/zero-vtes/blob/main/docs/rules-qa.md#batch-66--play-feedback-the-as-announced-window-stealth-a-block-can-still-reach-owner-2026-10-12));
{Gwenllian Arwyn}'s -1 intercept "while stealth is needed" ([rules-qa
15.2](https://github.com/lionel-panhaleux/zero-vtes/blob/main/docs/rules-qa.md#batch-15--printed-abilities-outside-combat)).

**Where the document stands.** §3.2.2: "Such a card may be played by a minion that is not attempting to block" — the
ruling's side, narrower than it. If the ruling stands: "Such a card needs no block attempt under way…" ([^3-2-3] cites
LSJ 20020612).

## 2. Is a hunt at full capacity a successful hunt?

**The question.** A vampire at capacity hunts unblocked; the blood drains off at once. Has he "successfully hunted"
({Harvest Rites}, {Hunger Moon}), and is it {Abactor}'s "successful resolution"? Does a hunting ground used on him spend
his one hunting ground?

**The record.**

- [RBK hunt]: the excess "is immediately returned to the blood bank".
- [LSJ 20050720-2](https://usenet.krcg.org/t/1vbIkgvKDJ0/#m1): "The hunt is not successful if no blood is gained" (the
  case was {Foul Blood} reducing the gain to zero).
- [LSJ 20090411](https://usenet.krcg.org/t/2aJf9bN7ZGc/#m6): {Abactor}'s "successful resolution" is the same test.
- [TOM 19951212-3](https://usenet.krcg.org/t/cYwUsviEhr4/#m2) {Triole's Revenge}: burns "if the benefit of the hunt is
  reduced to zero".

**The owner's reading.** A hunt at capacity is successful: "the fact the blood bleeds off does not stop the hunt from
getting the blood" ([rules-qa
89.1](https://github.com/lionel-panhaleux/zero-vtes/blob/main/docs/rules-qa.md#batch-89--round-20-the-cards-players-asked-for-the-two-player-lists-last-two-readings-graded-claude-2026-10-23-owner-2026-10-23-891-corrected-892-confirmed),
corrected 2026-10-23); yet "at capacity or diseased, no blood hunt" for {Abactor} ([rules-qa
63.2](https://github.com/lionel-panhaleux/zero-vtes/blob/main/docs/rules-qa.md#batch-63--legacy-cards-for-twda-decks-round-3-the-owners-list-implemented-readings-2026-10-11-owner-all-confirmed)).
LSJ 20090411 makes these one test, so both cannot stand. A hunting ground used at capacity "counts as its one hunting
ground" ([rules-qa
7.2](https://github.com/lionel-panhaleux/zero-vtes/blob/main/docs/rules-qa.md#batch-7--library-card-contests-hunting-grounds)),
which pulls against §1.2.1's "a limited use granting nothing spends no limit".

**Where the document stands.** §3.7.2.1 "with zero blood gained the hunt is not successful"; §3.7.2.2/§3.7.2.1 {Triole's
Revenge} "fires although the hunt gained nothing" (implies no gain at capacity); §3.4.1 {Abactor} "unblocked *and* blood
gained". §3.7.2.4: "the excess returns to the blood bank as normal".

## 3. Has a card "put in play" by an effect been played?

**The question.** Is an ally {Summon History} puts in play "played", for "only one … can be played" limits, "when
played" triggers and play counts?

**The record.**

- owner-rulings.md B: a card played "not in the normal fashion" has still been played (equip, recruit, employ).
- [LSJ 20060209](https://usenet.krcg.org/t/ZuOfZorIhhU/#m4) {Flaming Candle}: an effect can still "put it into play" if
  it is not an equip action (the cases are moves).
- [ANK 20201229](https://usenet.krcg.org/t/vekn-78959/#m1) G00131: under {Piper}-type
  effects "the card is not 'played' by the minion".
- [LSJ 20051116-2](https://usenet.krcg.org/t/5Bovsb8I6R0/#m1) {Abombwe}: requirements ignored "if the card is not played
  in the normal fashion (eg. diablerie or {Bima})".

**The owner's reading.** "Only an effect that says *put in play* does not play the card" (rules-qa Default readings;
[40.10](https://github.com/lionel-panhaleux/zero-vtes/blob/main/docs/rules-qa.md#batch-40--events),
[44.4](https://github.com/lionel-panhaleux/zero-vtes/blob/main/docs/rules-qa.md#batch-44--legacy-cards-for-tournament-decks-slice-16a),
77.3).

**Where the document stands.** §1.8.2 "A card brought into play by another card's effect has still been played";
Appendix A "put … in play": "the card has still been played"; §1.11.1 likewise.

  20170326](https://usenet.krcg.org/t/vekn-75635/#m5) {Gift of
  Bellona}: "Can be used when fetching a weapon from another minion".

  20221103](https://usenet.krcg.org/t/vekn-80131/#m2)

  20090506]: put in play, it gets no cards.

**The owner's reading.** The take is not "an equip action" for {Hag's Wrinkles} ([rules-qa
78.28](https://github.com/lionel-panhaleux/zero-vtes/blob/main/docs/rules-qa.md#batch-78--round-15-the-2023-07-01-promo-set-the-twdas-steps-18-readings-graded-claude-2026-10-17-owner-2026-10-17-781-confirmed-7832-and-7838-corrected-7828-and-7854-completed-the-ambers-confirmed),

([rules-qa

12.2](https://github.com/lionel-panhaleux/zero-vtes/blob/main/docs/rules-qa.md#batch-12--readings-of-stored-cards)).

**Where the document stands.** §3.7.3.2: the take is "an action to equip with each of them", {Unlicensed Taxicab}, {Gift
